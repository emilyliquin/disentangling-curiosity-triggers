In this research, we are investigating what makes people curious about why something happened. This explanation-seeking curiosity motivates much of human learning - as a result of our search for explanations, we can gain new insight into an unusual or surprising event, which may lead us to revise our beliefs about the world. In this experiment, we are testing how the sequence of information received over time impacts curiosity.
